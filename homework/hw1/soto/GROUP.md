## How this folder relates to the others

- The break scripts, reports and SECS design in this folder come from the group's
  implementation, which Mauricio Mantilla submitted first in
  [`homework/hw1/mantilla/`](../mantilla/). This folder is James Soto's copy of that
  work, with the paths in the READMEs updated from `mantilla/` to `soto/`.
- `AI_LOG.md`: Tasks 1-5 were logged by Mantilla and describe his sessions.
  Task 6 was logged by Soto and covers his verification of the deliverable
  against the rubric (running the four break scripts, `verify_plaintext_evidence.py`
  and `test_timing_counts.py` and checking the outputs against the report).
